Rules

Orange marks only power and the active choice: the slider fill, the selected mode ring, a running power button.
Selection is solid ink: black in light, near-white in dark. Pills slide to the new choice.
Cards are frosted glass with large radii. Air particles flow behind them, so the blur has something to show.
Flat colour only. The background is slow drifting breeze lines, never a gradient fill, wash or glowing blob.
